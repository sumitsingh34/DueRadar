# DueRadar

**See what's coming before it's due.**

DueRadar is a free, open-source app that makes sure you never miss a renewal, price increase or
expiry. Keep track of subscriptions, memberships, insurance, phone plans, domains and card annual
fees in one place.

## Features

- What you pay each month and each year, at a glance
- Reminders before each renewal or expiry, at the time you choose
- Price history, and a heads-up when a price goes up
- Quick-add for 40+ common services, and search
- Export a backup and restore it on a new phone
- Works offline, with no account

## Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
