# DueRadar privacy policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

DueRadar is a free, open-source app for keeping track of subscriptions, renewals, warranties,
home and car maintenance, licenses, appointments, documents and other dates that matter. It is
designed to work without collecting anything about you.

## In short

- No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Everything you enter stays on your device.
- Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else unless you choose to export and share it.

## What the app stores

The items you add (names, costs, dates, companies and notes), their price history, the vehicles
and homes you add with their odometer readings, a history of when you did each task, and your
settings are saved in a database on your device. Receipt photos you add are copied into the app's
private storage on your device. None of this is uploaded anywhere, and Android's automatic backup
to your Google account doesn't include it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Documents

You can keep track of documents such as a passport or ID card: their expiry date and other details
you enter. Adding photos of a document is optional. Document photos are encrypted on your device
(AES-256) with a key that is kept in your phone's secure storage and never leaves the device. If
you move to a new phone, the photos can only come along in a backup you export yourself.

## Camera and photos

DueRadar only uses the camera or your photos when you add a photo of a receipt or a document.
Taking a photo asks for camera permission. Choosing a photo opens your phone's photo picker, so
DueRadar only sees the photo you pick, not the rest of your photos.

## App lock

If you turn on the app lock, DueRadar asks your phone to confirm it's you with your fingerprint,
face or screen lock (PIN, pattern or password). This check happens entirely in your phone's
operating system. DueRadar never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

## Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone's operating system, on the device itself. No push service
or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in your phone's
settings.

## Backups and exports

When you export a backup (a .json file, which includes your receipt photos) or a spreadsheet (a
.csv file), the app creates the file and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes,
for example Google Drive, email or your files, and that service's own privacy policy then applies.
If you have document photos, the app asks whether to include them in the backup. Photos in a
backup file are not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a backup reads only the file
you pick.

## Permissions

DueRadar asks for permission to send notifications, so it can remind you before due dates, and for
the camera only when you take a photo. If you turn on the app lock, it uses your phone's
fingerprint or face unlock. It does not use your location, contacts, microphone or advertising ID.

## Children

DueRadar does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
