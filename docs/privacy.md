# DueRadar privacy policy

_Last updated: October 5, 2026_

DueRadar is a free, open-source app for keeping track of subscriptions, renewals, warranties,
home and car maintenance, and expiry dates. It is designed to work without collecting anything about you.

## In short

- No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Everything you enter stays on your device.
- Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else unless you choose to export and share it.

## What the app stores

The items you add (names, costs, dates, companies and notes), their price history, the vehicles
and homes you add with their odometer readings, a history of when you did each task, and your
settings are saved in a database on your device. Receipt photos you add are copied into the app's
private storage on your device. None of this is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Camera and photos

DueRadar only uses the camera or your photos when you add a receipt to an item. Taking a photo
asks for camera permission. Choosing a photo opens your phone's photo picker, so DueRadar only sees
the photo you pick, not the rest of your photos.

## Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone's operating system, on the device itself. No push service
or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in your phone's
settings.

## Backups and exports

When you export a backup (a .json file, which includes your receipt photos) or a spreadsheet (a
.csv file), the app creates the file and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes,
for example Google Drive, email or your files, and that service's own privacy policy then applies.
Restoring a backup reads only the file you pick.

## Permissions

DueRadar asks for permission to send notifications, so it can remind you before due dates, and for
the camera only when you take a photo of a receipt. It does not use your location, contacts,
microphone or advertising ID.

## Children

DueRadar does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
