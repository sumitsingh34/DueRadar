# DueRadar privacy policy

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

DueRadar is a free, open-source app for keeping track of subscriptions, renewals and expiry dates.
It is designed to work without collecting anything about you.

## In short

- No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Everything you enter stays on your device.
- Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else unless you choose to export and share it.

## What the app stores

The items you add (names, costs, dates, companies and notes), their price history and your
settings are saved in a database on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the
app deletes them.

## Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your phone's operating system, on the device itself. No push service
or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in your phone's
settings.

## Backups and exports

When you export a backup (a .json file) or a spreadsheet (a .csv file), the app creates the file
and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example Google Drive, email or
your files, and that service's own privacy policy then applies. Restoring a backup reads only the
file you pick.

## Permissions

DueRadar asks for permission to send notifications, so it can remind you before due dates. It
does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

## Children

DueRadar does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
