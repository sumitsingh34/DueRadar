DueRadar shows everything you pay for again and again, and reminds you before it's due.

Add your subscriptions, memberships, insurance, phone and internet plans, domains and card annual fees. DueRadar adds up what they cost each month and each year, shows what's coming up in the next 30 days, and sends a reminder before each renewal.

WHAT YOU GET
• Monthly and yearly totals at a glance
• Reminders 30, 7 or 1 day before a due date, at the time you choose
• Price history for every item, and a heads-up when a price goes up
• Warranties: purchase date, store, price and a photo of the receipt, with a reminder before the warranty ends
• Quick-add for 50+ popular services and products: type "Net" and pick Netflix
• Auto-renewals move forward on their own. Manual renewals stay flagged until you mark them renewed
• Search, with edit and delete right from the list
• Your currency: ₹, $, €, £ and more
• Back up to a file (receipt photos included) and restore on a new phone, or export a spreadsheet (CSV)
• Light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone and works offline. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you export it yourself.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
DueRadar is free, with no in-app purchases. The source code is on GitHub: github.com/sumitsingh34/DueRadar
